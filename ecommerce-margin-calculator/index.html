Ecommerce margin calculator

Sale price minus product cost, advertising, and the platform percent you enter.

Notes, limits & FAQ

How this figure is calculated

The margin starts at the sale price minus the product cost. Advertising and the platform percent you enter are then subtracted. What remains is the contribution on that order. Divide by the sale price for the margin percent.

What this page leaves out

Returns, payment-processing fees, and shipping are included only when you put them in the cost or advertising fields. It is not a forecast of how many orders will sell.

Worked example

A $40 sale with a $15 product cost, $8 of advertising, and a 15% platform fee of $6 leaves $11. The margin on the sale price is 27.5%. Raise the advertising and the $11 falls by the same amount.
