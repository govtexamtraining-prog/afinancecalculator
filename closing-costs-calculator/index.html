Closing cost calculator

Adds the fee amounts you enter for a US-style closing. It does not calculate Canadian land transfer tax or title insurance.

Worked example

A 1% origination charge on a $360,000 loan is $3,600. Add the flat title and escrow amounts you type and the page shows the cash due at closing from those lines alone.

How this figure is calculated

The total is the sum of the fee amounts you type: lender charges, title, escrow, and any other lines on the form. Nothing is looked up from a county fee schedule. Percent fields, where the form has them, are that percent times the price or loan you entered.

What this page leaves out

This is a US-style list of fees you enter. It does not calculate Canadian land transfer tax, title insurance, or a provincial welcome tax. Prepaid property tax and insurance are included only when you type them.
