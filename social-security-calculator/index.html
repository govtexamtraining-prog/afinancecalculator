Social Security calculator

Compares US claiming ages from the benefit amount you enter. It does not look up your Social Security statement, and it is not Canada Pension Plan or OAS.

Worked example

If the benefit you type at full retirement age is $2,000 a month, claiming earlier shows a smaller monthly amount and claiming later shows a larger one. The comparison is only as good as the benefit you entered.

How this figure is calculated

You enter the monthly benefit at one claiming age. The page scales that benefit to the other claiming ages using the early and delayed factors in the tool, so you can compare the monthly amounts. It does not contact the Social Security Administration and it does not read a statement.

What this page leaves out

This is United States Social Security. It is not the Canada Pension Plan or Old Age Security. Spousal benefits, the earnings test, and taxation of benefits are not calculated.
