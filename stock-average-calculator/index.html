Stock average cost calculator

Combines the share lots you enter into one average cost.

Worked example

100 shares at $40 and 100 shares at $60 cost $10,000 together. The average cost is $50 a share. Selling above $50 is a gain on that average. Selling below it is a loss.

How this figure is calculated

Average cost is the total amount paid divided by the total shares. Each lot contributes shares times price. A later purchase at a higher price pulls the average up. A later purchase at a lower price pulls it down. The page combines the lots you enter into one average.

What this page leaves out

Commissions are included only if you folded them into the prices you typed. The tax on a sale is the capital-gains page, not this one.
