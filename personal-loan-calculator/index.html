Personal loan calculator

Personal loan calculator for monthly payment and interest on the amount, rate, and term you enter.

Worked example

Borrowing $10,000 at 12% for 36 months produces a fixed payment that covers interest and principal. The total of the 36 payments is more than $10,000 by the interest. A 0% rate makes the payment the amount divided by the months.

How this figure is calculated

The payment is the standard monthly amortization of the amount, rate, and term you enter. Interest is the payment times the number of months, minus the amount borrowed. An origination fee is part of the cost only when the form asks for it or you have added it to the amount.

What this page leaves out

A late fee, a variable rate, and credit insurance are not added. The APR a lender advertises may already include a fee that this payment ignores unless you enter it.

Questions

What does this personal loan calculator show?

Monthly payment and total interest for a personal loan from the amount, rate, and term you enter.

Is this the same as a loan payment calculator?

Yes for fixed personal loans. The general loan calculator page uses the same payment math. For a home loan use the mortgage calculator. For a vehicle use the auto loan calculator.

Does the personal loan calculator include origination fees?
