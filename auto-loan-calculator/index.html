Auto loan calculator

Free auto loan calculator and car payment calculator for monthly principal and interest. Tax, title, and dealer fees are not added unless already in the amount.

Worked example

A $30,000 loan at 7% for 60 months has a fixed monthly principal-and-interest payment. Sales tax is not added on top. If the amount you typed already includes tax and fees, those costs are inside the payment. If it does not, they are missing.

How this figure is calculated

The monthly payment uses the standard amortizing formula. The amount is the number you enter. The rate is divided by 12. The term is the number of months on the form. Total interest is the payment times the number of months, minus the amount borrowed.

What this page leaves out

Tax, title, and dealer fees are not added unless they are already in the amount. A down payment should be subtracted before you type the amount, or entered in the down-payment field when the form has one.

Questions

How do I estimate a car payment with an auto loan calculator?

Enter the financed amount, APR, and term in months. The page returns principal and interest. Tax and title are included only if already in the amount.

Is this a car loan calculator and car payment calculator?

Yes. It uses the same amortizing formula for auto loans. Lease vs buy is a separate page.

Does the car payment include insurance?

No. Insurance and registration are outside this payment unless you added them to the loan amount yourself.
