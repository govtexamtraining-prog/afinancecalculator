Rent vs buy calculator

Rent vs buy calculator comparing five years of rent with principal, interest, and a sale cost. It leaves out repairs, tax, and insurance unless you add them.

Worked example

If rent is $2,200 a month and does not change, five years of rent is $132,000 before moving costs. The buy side depends on the price, the down payment, and the rate you type. The verdict is the difference between those two paths over the years on the form, not a lifetime forecast.

How this figure is calculated

The page compares five years of rent with the principal and interest on a purchase, plus a sale cost you can read on the form. Rent can grow at the rate you enter. The buy side uses the home price, down payment, and mortgage rate on the form. Any investment return you enter is applied only to the case the form labels.

What this page leaves out

Repairs, property tax, insurance, and homeowners association fees are left out unless a field on the form asks for them. It is not a full cost of owning, and it is not tax advice on the mortgage-interest deduction.

Questions

Is it better to rent or buy a home?

It depends on rent, the mortgage payment, how long you stay, and what you do with the down payment you would otherwise invest. This page compares five years of those cash paths from the numbers you enter. It does not decide the right choice for every city.

Does this rent vs buy calculator include property tax?

Only if you add tax, insurance, or repairs in the fields the form provides. The default comparison is rent versus principal, interest, and a sale cost. It is not a full PITI budget.

Can I use this for the US and Canada?

Yes for a cash comparison. It does not run the Canadian mortgage stress test, land transfer tax, or US mortgage-interest deduction rules by itself.
