# aFinanceCalculator - Universal 20-in-1 Global Financial Computing Engine

This repository contains the complete production-grade source code for **aFinanceCalculator**, a high-performance **20-in-1 financial workspace**.

The entire software architecture runs strictly on high-performance, client-side mechanics (Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, and Tailwind CSS Layout Engines). It requires **zero server-side databases, zero API keys, requires absolute zero maintenance, and costs $0 to host forever**.

---

## 🗂️ Workspace Architecture

```text
├── index.html          # Structural UI DOM Node Mapping & Layout Frame
├── css/
│   └── styles.css      # Visual Workspace Tweaks & Dark/Light Themes
└── js/
    └── mechanics.js    # 20-in-1 Computing Engine, Charts, CSV Exporter & Scenarios
```

## 🚀 The 20 Financial Computing Engines

1. **🏠 Mortgage Liability Console**: Real-time capital mortgage outlays with 15/30-year amortization toggles, LTV, interest totals, and visual breakdown charts.
2. **💼 Contractor Invoicing & Tax Matrix**: Gross invoice calculations, isolated tax buffer reserves, and net retained yields.
3. **📈 Campaign Enterprise ROI Metrics**: ROAS multiple, net capital profit, and net investment return percentage.
4. **⚡ Crypto Futures Leverage & Liquidation Matrix**: Long/Short leveraged contract positions with liquidation boundary danger gauge.
5. **💰 Compound Interest Wealth Multiplier Suite**: Multi-year compound wealth realization targets with real-time SVG exponential curve.
6. **🏢 Commercial Asset Cap Rate Matrix**: Net Operating Income (NOI) appraisal and market capitalization rate computation.
7. **⏱️ Wage Hourly to Salary Converter Engine**: Instant conversion across hourly, weekly, monthly, and annualized compensation.
8. **📊 Startup Venture Break-Even Level**: Unit volume sales requirements, contribution margin, and gross break-even revenue.
9. **💳 Credit Card Liability Elimination Engine**: Amortization debt discharge schedule, interest cost drain, and repayment bounds.
10. **📉 Equity Asset Cost Averaging Suite**: Multi-tranche weighted average purchase price and aggregate position asset cost.
11. **🏘️ Residential Housing Analysis Framework**: Monthly and 5-year cumulative cash flow variance between renting and purchasing.
12. **🛒 Retail Commerce Gross Profit Margin Suite**: Procurement cost margins, markup percentages, and net profit premiums.
13. **🚗 Auto Capital Financing Estimator Matrix**: Vehicle loan liability, total financing interest, and monthly installments.
14. **🎓 Student Loan Payoff Horizon Matrix**: Education loan amortization schedule, monthly payment, and total lifetime interest outlay.
15. **💵 50/30/20 Portfolio Allocation Framework**: Balanced budgeting partition into Essential Needs (50%), Flexible Wants (30%), and Retained Savings (20%) with segmented bar.
16. **🔢 Simple Interest Contract Ledger Core**: Promissory notes, pure generated yield, and total aggregate payback valuation.
17. **🪙 Stock Dividend Yield Console**: Annual and monthly equity holdings dividend distribution matrices.
18. **🎈 Cash Inflation Eroder Suite**: Macro purchasing power degradation modeling with 5, 10, 15, and 20-year milestones.
19. **🗓️ Systematic Monthly Investment (SIP) Planner**: Recurring mutual fund/SIP accumulation with dual-curve SVG growth charts.
20. **🔄 Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) Computer**: Geometric annualized growth rates and capital multiples across assets.

## 🌟 Advanced FinTech-Grade Features

1. **📊 Interactive SVG Micro-Charts**:
   - Dynamic Principal vs. Lifetime Interest breakdown bar.
   - Real-time SVG Exponential Growth Curve for Compound Interest.
   - Dual-Path Cumulative Investment vs. Compounding Corpus Area Chart for SIP Planner.
   - 3-Color Segmented Allocation Bar for 50/30/20 Budgeting.
   - Macro Inflation Milestone Decay Indicators (5, 10, 15, 20 Years).
   - Crypto Liquidation Danger Proximity Gauge.

2. **📑 Expandable Amortization Schedule & CSV/Excel Exporter**:
   - Comprehensive drawer modal for Mortgage, Auto Loan, and Student Loans.
   - Tabbed view: **Annual Summary** (years 1..30) and **Monthly Breakdown** (months 1..360).
   - Instant RFC 4180 **Download CSV** generation for Excel / Google Sheets.

3. **⚖️ "What-If" Sensitivity & Extra Payment Simulator**:
   - Direct 30-Yr vs. 15-Yr comparative interest savings badge.
   - Extra Monthly Principal Simulator (+$100, +$250, +$500/mo) calculating exact time shaved off and interest saved.

4. **🔗 Shareable Scenario Deep-Linking**:
   - Encodes any active configuration into URL hash parameters.
   - One-click copy generates persistent client-shareable URLs.

5. **💾 LocalStorage Auto-Save & History Drawer**:
   - Automatically persists all user inputs across browser sessions.
   - Slide-out **Calculation History Drawer** logs recent calculations with instant "Jump" buttons.

6. **🌓 Dark / Light Theme Mode**:
   - Seamless header toggle switching between Slate Dark and Crisp Light modes.

7. **⌨️ Pro Productivity Shortcuts**:
   - `Ctrl + K` or `/` focuses the search box instantly.
   - `Esc` closes active modals and drawers.
   - Clean printable stylesheet with isolated focused engine printing (`Ctrl + P` or top bar / card Print buttons prints ONLY the active/focused financial engine).
