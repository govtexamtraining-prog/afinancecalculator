How much house can I afford calculator

Home affordability calculator estimating purchase price from income, debts, and the housing share you set. Not a Canadian stress test or lender pre-approval.

Worked example

A household that can put $2,200 a month toward principal and interest, at 6.2% for 30 years, supports a loan near $360,000. With $90,000 down, the price is near $450,000. Change the rate or the term and both numbers move.

How this figure is calculated

The payment the form allows is the housing share you set, times gross monthly income, minus the monthly debts you enter. That payment is turned back into a loan amount with the standard mortgage formula at the rate and term you enter. Adding the down payment gives a purchase price.

What this page leaves out

This is not a lender pre-approval. It is not the Canadian mortgage stress test. Taxes, insurance, and mortgage insurance are outside the payment unless you have already reduced the housing budget to leave room for them.

Questions

How much house can I afford?

This home affordability calculator turns income, debts, rate, term, and down payment into an estimated purchase price. It is a planning estimate, not a lender pre-approval.

Does this house affordability calculator include taxes and insurance?

Not inside the payment unless you have already left room for them in the housing share you set. The tool focuses on principal and interest capacity.

Is this the Canadian mortgage stress test?

No. Use a lender or broker for the federal qualifying rate. This page is a US-style income-and-debt estimate.
