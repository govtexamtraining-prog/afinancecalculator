Airbnb income calculator

Nightly rate times the occupancy you enter, minus the mortgage and operating costs you enter. It is not a forecast of bookings.

Worked example

A $200 night at 60% occupancy is 219 occupied nights and $43,800 of gross bookings before fees. Subtract the platform percent, mortgage, and operating bills you type to reach the cash left.

How this figure is calculated

Gross booking revenue is the nightly rate times occupied nights. Occupied nights are the occupancy percent you enter times 365. Platform fees, the mortgage payment, and the operating costs you enter are subtracted. Net monthly cash is that annual result divided by 12. Margin is net divided by gross.

What this page leaves out

It is not a forecast of bookings. Seasonality, cleaning, local lodging tax, and empty months appear only when you put them in the occupancy or cost fields.

Questions

How do I calculate Airbnb income?

Multiply the nightly rate by occupied nights. Occupied nights are occupancy percent times 365. Subtract platform fees, the mortgage, and operating costs. This page does that from the numbers you type.

What occupancy rate should I use?

Use a rate you believe for that market and season, not a best-case month. Empty weeks lower cash flow quickly. The tool does not forecast bookings.

Does this include cleaning fees and lodging tax?

Only if you put them in the operating cost or fee fields. They are not added automatically.
