Profit margin calculator

Price minus cost, as a margin and as a markup.

Worked example

A product that costs $60 and sells for $100 has a $40 profit. Margin is 40%. Markup is 66.7%, because $40 / $60 is two-thirds. Pricing from a desired margin uses price = cost / (1 − margin).

How this figure is calculated

Margin is (price − cost) / price. Markup is (price − cost) / cost. They are different denominators. A 50% markup on cost is not a 50% margin on price. The page shows both from the cost and price you enter.

What this page leaves out

Overhead, shipping, and advertising are in the cost only if you included them in the cost you typed. Tax on the profit is not removed.
