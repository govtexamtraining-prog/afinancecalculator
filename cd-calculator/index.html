Notes, limits & FAQ

How this figure is calculated

Maturity ≈ principal × (1 + APY/n)^(n×years), where n is the compounding frequency you select.

What this page leaves out

Early withdrawal penalties and promotional APYs are not priced unless you change the rate.

Worked example

A $10,000 CD at 4.5% APY for 12 months with monthly compounding earns interest to maturity on that schedule.

Questions

Is this a live CD rate?

No. Enter the APY from the bank. Pair with the CD ladder tool for staggered maturities.
