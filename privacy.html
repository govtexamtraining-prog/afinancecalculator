Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Compliant with Google AdSense Policies, GDPR (EU/UK), and CCPA/CPRA (California)

🛡️ 100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee (Zero-Server Architecture)

Unlike conventional financial web apps that transmit your salary, loan amounts, mortgage balance, and debt figures to remote cloud servers or databases, aFinanceCalculator executes all mathematical algorithms entirely inside your local web browser runtime (HTML5/JavaScript). We have zero user registration, zero remote databases, and we never collect, store, transmit, or sell your private financial computation data.

1. Introduction

aFinanceCalculator ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website located at https://afinancecalculator.com. This Privacy Policy informs visitors and users ("you") regarding our policies concerning the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when using our financial computing engines, and the choices you have associated with that data.

2. Financial Calculation Inputs & LocalStorage

When you input data into our 50 financial engines (e.g., mortgage balances, down payments, interest rates, tax estimates, cryptocurrency leverage, or debt amounts):

  • All mathematical computations happen instantly on your client device using native JavaScript.
  • If you choose to use the "Save Scenario" or "Calculation History" features, your preferences and inputs are stored strictly in your browser's private localStorage space.
  • You can erase all saved calculation history at any time with a single click using the "Clear All" button in the History Drawer.
  • No server-side database exists to receive or record your calculations.
