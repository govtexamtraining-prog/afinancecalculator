Dividend yield calculator

Annual dividend divided by price. It does not predict whether the dividend continues.

Worked example

A $2 annual dividend on a $50 share is a 4% yield. Two hundred shares at that dividend pay $400 a year before tax. The page does not assume the dividend is raised.

How this figure is calculated

Dividend yield is the annual dividend per share divided by the price per share. The dollar income is the annual dividend times the number of shares. The yield falls when the price rises and the dividend stays the same.

What this page leaves out

It does not predict whether the dividend continues, and it does not subtract withholding tax. Price change is not part of the yield.
