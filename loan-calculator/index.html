Loan calculator

Free loan calculator for monthly payment and total interest on the amount, rate, and term you enter. Use the mortgage or auto loan pages when the product is a home or vehicle loan.

Worked example

Borrowing $10,000 at 12% for 36 months produces a fixed payment that covers interest and principal. The total of the 36 payments is more than $10,000 by the interest. A 0% rate makes the payment the amount divided by the months.

How this figure is calculated

The payment is the standard monthly amortization of the amount, rate, and term you enter. Interest is the payment times the number of months, minus the amount borrowed. This is the same amortizing loan math used for many personal loans.

What this page leaves out

A late fee, a variable rate, and credit insurance are not added. For a home mortgage use the mortgage calculator. For a car loan use the auto loan calculator.

Questions

What does this free loan calculator show?

Monthly payment and total interest from the amount, rate, and term you enter.

Is this also an amortization calculator?

Yes for a fixed amortizing loan payment. It does not print a full month-by-month schedule table on this page.

Should I use mortgage calculator or loan calculator?

Use the mortgage calculator for a home loan with US monthly compounding. Use this loan calculator for a general or personal loan payment. Use the auto loan calculator for a car payment.
