Notes, limits & FAQ

How this figure is calculated

The monthly principal and interest payment is M = P × [i(1+i)^n] / [(1+i)^n − 1]. P is the price minus the down payment. For a United States fixed mortgage, i is the annual rate divided by 12, and n is the number of months. A 30-year loan uses 360 months. Optional property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI, and HOA fields are added on top of P&I to estimate a full PITI housing payment.

What this page leaves out

Escrow amounts are estimates you type — not county tax bills or carrier quotes. The rate is compounded monthly (US fixed-rate convention). This is not a lender approval. Use the Canada mortgage page for semi-annual compounding and CMHC premium math.

Worked example

A $450,000 home with $90,000 down leaves a $360,000 loan. At 6.2% for 30 years principal and interest is $2,204.89 a month. Adding $375 tax, $140 insurance, and $0 PMI/HOA brings estimated PITI to $2,719.89.

Questions

What is the monthly payment on a $360,000 mortgage at 6.2% for 30 years?

Principal and interest is $2,204.89 a month. Add your tax, insurance, PMI, and HOA fields for a PITI total.

Does this mortgage calculator include taxes and insurance?

Yes as optional monthly fields. Enter property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI, and HOA to see estimated PITI on top of principal and interest.

Is this free mortgage calculator also a loan calculator or amortization calculator?

Yes for principal and interest. Use the dedicated amortization calculator for a month-by-month schedule, or the personal/auto loan pages for non-mortgage products.

Is this for the United States or Canada?

This page uses US monthly compounding. For a Canadian quote compounded semi-annually, use the Canada mortgage calculator.
